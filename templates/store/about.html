Our Mission

To give Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) a chance to earn income by providing an online marketplace where they can sell their handmade products. Through LIKHALAYA, we hope to give PDLs more opportunities, promote second chances, and make a positive impact in their lives and the community.

Our Vision

To create a future where Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) have equal opportunities to share their talents and improve their lives through honest work. We want LIKHALAYA to be a platform that promotes hope, supports rehabilitation, and inspires people to believe in second chances.

Handcrafted with Heart and Purpose

Carigara District Jail, under the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP) in Leyte, provides livelihood programs that help Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) learn valuable skills and create quality handmade products. These include woven crafts, woodwork, metal crafts, and other locally made items that reflect their creativity, hard work, and dedication.

LIKHALAYA comes from the Filipino word "likha," which means to create. It represents the creativity, hope, and determination of PDL artisans as they turn their skills into meaningful products. Through this platform, every handcrafted item becomes more than just a product—it becomes a symbol of growth, rehabilitation, and the opportunity for a better future.
